# time off 3

An interactive album experience built in Unity 6. Explore an abandoned music studio while listening to 8 lofi hip hop & funk instrumentals.

## Concept

You are Patchy — a cute robot made from spare digital electronics and audio equipment, with a breadboard chest wired with capacitors, resistors, and jumper wires from a half-finished filter design. Your home is an abandoned state-of-the-art music studio that produced some of the biggest hits of the 70s and 80s. Everything is intact and functional, just covered in layers of dust and cobwebs.

Each room of the studio corresponds to one track on the album. Walk through the live room, control room, vocal booth, equipment closet, tape machine room, lounge, echo chamber, and rooftop — or just press play and let the album wash over you.

## How to Play

- **Listen** — The album plays automatically. You don't have to do anything.
- **Explore** — Walk around with WASD, look with mouse. No objectives.
- **Interact** — Find cables to plug in, switches to flip, faders to slide. Pure visual feedback.
- **Three modes** — Pick a track (Menu), walk between rooms (Connected), or listen straight through (Sequential).

## Requirements
